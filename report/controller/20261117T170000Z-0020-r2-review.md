read atomic with a concurrent update to the job row. Service-owned units
`network/services.py:1350–1375` only check at entry; even scope's final check
leaves a check-to-commit window without a shared row lock/CAS. The internal
correction reviewer called this CORR-REV-03 theoretical/nonblocking; root
now reproduces it on the **actual discovery service**.

Gate immediately after its real post-I/O ownership guard → independent
connection expires/reconciles the job to uncertain → resume object unit:
**DiscoveryObject1 and snapshot succeeded commit while job stays uncertain**.
The original loss-before-guard test passes; it does not cover this schedule.

**Minimum:** serialize guarded domain commit against ownership-changing
updates using the existing job row/transaction (row lock/CAS or equivalently
sound mechanism), check valid expected state/token/epoch/lease and stop on
loss. Apply consistently to scope and service-owned units, including
finalize_verification. Keep I/O outside DB transactions and test lock order.
**Closure A01/A02/A05:** this schedule either prevents ownership transfer
until the short unit commits or rolls the losing unit back; no post-loss
domain write/snapshot success/new send. Preserve terminal-envelope CAS and
all old passing probes. Do not merely add another unprotected SELECT.
