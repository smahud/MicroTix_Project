### REV-1711-05 — original enrollment-lock schedule closed; credential wait still exposed

The R3 change is effective for the exact earlier schedule. In
`backend/domains/network/services.py:1284`, finalization acquires the enrollment
row, then `:1306–1307` invokes the fresh authority callback before creating
Router/credential effects. A revoke committed while this enrollment lock is
held by another connection is visible to this new read. Keep this correction
and the original post-I/O authority check at `:1275–1276`.

A later potentially blocking write is still unprotected against the same
failure class. The service updates `SstpAccount` at `:1344–1345` or
`WireGuardPeer` at `:1348–1349` **after** its last authority read. PostgreSQL
can wait arbitrarily on an existing credential row lock. Membership
revocation can commit independently during that wait. After the lock is
released, finalization activates the credential and completes the Router
transaction. The sole final callback at `:1351–1355` is ownership-only
`ctx.guard_commit`; `backend/domains/jobs/engine.py:247–278` checks the job
lease/state, not current actor/membership permission. No later authority
check exists in this unit. `check_current_authority`'s membership SELECT at
`engine.py:582` does not serialize a concurrent revocation.
